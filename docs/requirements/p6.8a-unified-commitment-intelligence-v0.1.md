# P6.8A Requirements

Nexuss shall:

1. Connect one Google identity through desktop OAuth with PKCE.
2. Encrypt OAuth material with Windows DPAPI.
3. Request only Gmail, Calendar, and Contacts read scopes.
4. Bound message, event, calendar, and contact retrieval.
5. Correlate official Google reads with trusted mobile signals.
6. Extract only evidence-backed requests, promises, and responses needed.
7. Normalize explicit deadlines without inventing dates.
8. Detect overlapping calendar events.
9. Propose focus blocks without modifying the calendar.
10. Route natural-language commitment questions.
11. Block email, messaging, contact, and calendar writes.
12. Never expose access or refresh tokens in responses or logs.
