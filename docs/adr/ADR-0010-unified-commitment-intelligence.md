# ADR-0010: Unified Commitment Intelligence

## Decision

Correlate official Google Workspace reads with trusted Android communication
signals in one evidence-bound commitment graph.

## Rationale

Email, meetings, contacts, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram, and phone notifications
describe the same real-world obligations. Isolated summaries lose deadlines,
dependencies, unanswered requests, and schedule conflicts.

## Authority boundary

P6.8A is read-only. Focus blocks, replies, meeting changes, and messages are
proposals only. External provider writes require a later exact payload-bound
phone approval and provider-verification layer.
